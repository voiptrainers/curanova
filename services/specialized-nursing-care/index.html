Specialized Nursing Care

Specialized Nursing Care done properly

Trained and compassionate nursing care for your loved one's every need.

Book this service
Overview

What this programme actually includes

Our nursing team runs three shifts with written handover, so care never pauses. Every procedure follows infection-control protocol and is charted for review.

Nurses are trained in bedsore prevention, safe patient handling and early warning signs, and are supervised by a senior nursing lead.

Benefits

  • Injections, IV therapy and infusion management
  • Advanced wound, bedsore and ulcer dressing
  • Catheter, Ryle's tube and colostomy care
  • Tracheostomy and suction management
  • Vitals charting every shift
  • Structured handover and family reporting

Who needs it

  • Bedridden and post-stroke patients
  • Post-operative and post-ICU patients
  • Patients on long-term clinical procedures
  • Seniors needing constant nursing attention

Treatment process

How care is delivered and supervised

  1. 01

    Enquiry & visit

    Call us or visit the care home. Our advisor understands the condition and daily needs.

  2. 02

    Clinical assessment

    Our doctor and nursing lead assess the patient and prepare a written care plan.

  3. 03

    Admission & room

    A comfortable room is prepared with the equipment and diet the plan requires.

  4. 04

    Daily care delivery

    Care is delivered to protocol, charted every shift and reviewed by the doctor.

  5. 05

    Family updates

    Regular progress updates, reports and review meetings with the family.
