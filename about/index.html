About Curanova

We started because our own family couldn't find good care

More than a care home, we are family. Curanova exists to make clinically excellent, genuinely warm care the normal standard — not a lucky exception.

Our mission

To deliver hospital-standard clinical care in a homely setting, with the dignity, patience and continuity every family hopes for.

Our vision

A place where no family has to choose between expert medical care and the warmth of home.

Our promise

A safe place today, a better life everyday — with written care plans and an accountable team for every resident.

Our journey

Ten years of building one standard of care

  1. 16

    2016

    A single caregiver

    Founded after our own family struggled to find dependable care for a parent recovering from a stroke.

  2. 18

    2018

    Clinical academy launched

    In-house training and assessment programme created to standardise care quality across caregivers.

  3. 20

    2020

    Advanced medical support

    Added BiPAP, oxygen therapy and monitoring so critically ill residents could be cared for safely.

  4. 23

    2023

    Six cities, one standard

    Expanded with a supervisory nurse model so quality scaled with geography.

  5. 26

    2026

    250+ families later

    50+ professionals and a 98% family satisfaction score.

Care philosophy

Why families keep trusting us

Our model is deliberately supervision-heavy. Every caregiver reports to a nurse; every nurse reports to a clinical lead; every family gets one accountable coordinator.

Compassion first

Skill can be trained. Kindness is screened for at hiring and reinforced every single week.

Clinical integrity

Written protocols, charted care and honest escalation — even when the honest answer is inconvenient.

Quiet excellence

Premium is not marble lobbies. It is a nurse who arrives on time, prepared, every time.

Family partnership

Families are co-authors of the care plan, not spectators waiting for a phone update.
