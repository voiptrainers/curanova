Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 April 2026. This policy explains how we handle personal and health information.

Information we collect

Contact details, the patient's medical history relevant to care planning, care visit notes, and vitals recorded by our clinicians.

How we use it

To assess care needs, match a suitable caregiver, deliver and supervise care, share progress reports with authorised family members, and process payments.

Health data safeguards

Clinical records are encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted to the assigned care team on a need-to-know basis, and every access is logged.

Sharing

We share information only with the assigned care team, treating hospitals or doctors you nominate, and where required by law. We never sell personal or health data.

Retention

Clinical records are retained for the period required under applicable medical record regulations, after which they are securely destroyed.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, or withdraw consent for non-essential communication, by writing to privacy@curanovacarehome.com.
