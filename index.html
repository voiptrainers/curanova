How it works

Five steps from first call to confident recovery

No paperwork maze, no waiting. Most families complete admission within 24 hours of the assessment.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Call or WhatsApp us. A care advisor answers 24x7 and understands the requirement.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    Our doctor and nursing lead assess the patient and prepare a written care plan.

  3. 03

    Admission

    A comfortable room is prepared with the equipment, diet and support the plan needs.

  4. 04

    Daily care

    Doctors, nurses and therapists deliver care to protocol and chart every shift.

  5. 05

    Family updates

    Regular reports, health monitoring summaries and review meetings with the family.
